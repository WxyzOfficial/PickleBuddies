Features

Everything you need to play more pickleball

PickleBuddy is built from the ground up for the pickleball community — with all the tools you need to find, connect, and play.

Smart Match

Our intelligent algorithm recommends players based on skill level, playing style, schedule, and location. Find your perfect match in seconds.

Playing Now

Activate "I'm Playing Now" and appear on a live list. Nearby players looking for a game can instantly message you to hit the court.

In-App Messaging

Chat directly with other players, coordinate matches, and share court details — all in one place with real-time messaging.

Need Players

Create a game with exact open slots. Players join with one tap, and the post auto-updates to "Full" once all spots are taken.

Clubs & Community

Discover pickleball clubs near you, join communities, and grow your local pickleball network.

Safe & Respectful

Built-in reporting, community standards, and moderation keep PickleBuddy a safe space for players of all ages and skill levels.

How It Works

Start playing in 3 easy steps

No complicated setup. No subscriptions. Just create an account and start connecting with players.

1

Create Your Profile

Sign up in seconds with email or Google. Add your skill level, location, and playing preferences.

2

Find or Post a Game

Browse the feed, use Smart Match, or post your own request. Go live on Playing Now for instant games.

3

Connect & Play

Message players in-app, agree on a court, and hit the court. Leave a report if anyone misbehaves.
